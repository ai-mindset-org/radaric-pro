# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo content curators and newsletter operators, independent analysts, and Telegram digest editors processing 100–300 items per day. They work in fast daily triage sessions, moving between desktop and mobile. They are experts who value control, transparency, and trust over hand-holding — they want the tool to respect their judgment, not replace it.

## Product Purpose

Radaric filters hundreds of items per day against personalized heuristics that learn from feedback, so a curator's taste scales without their time scaling. It is a third category between aggregators (Feedly) and algorithmic feeds (Twitter/X): AI that learns to be *you*. Success means the curator trusts the filtering enough to spend minutes instead of hours, while the feedback knowledge base compounds into a durable, personal filter.

## Brand Personality

Calm, expert confidence with a distinct green signature. Quietly opinionated, fast, trustworthy. Carries editorial DNA from the author's own design-digest curation work — reading-first, respects the user's attention. Three words: **precise, green, confident.**

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS: purple gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates, emoji-laden empty states.
- Engagement-optimized consumer feeds: infinite-scroll dopamine, red-dot urgency, notification badges that nag.
- Sterile enterprise dashboards: gray-on-gray, chartjunk, dense forms with no rhythm.

It should feel like a craftsman's instrument, not a growth funnel.

## Design Principles

- **Green is the brand, not decoration.** The accent carries identity and state (selected, active, progress, success), used with intent rather than sprinkled.
- **Hierarchy through surface and depth, not borders.** Inherits the Radix borderless lineage; differentiates with background steps and a restrained elevation system.
- **The tool disappears into the task.** Density where it helps the curator move fast; calm and breathing room where the eye needs to rest.
- **Earned familiarity, distinctive finish.** Standard affordances (tabs, side nav, command-style toolbars) executed with a recognizable green-forward craft.
- **Trust through transparency.** AI reasoning, confidence scores, and processing state are always legible — the interface never hides what the model decided.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast across both light and dark themes (body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1). Minimum 44px touch targets. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation. Color is never the sole signal — status pairs color with an icon or label. Full Latin + Cyrillic support (Inter).
